Why Notes Rot: A Field Guide

Notes rot when nobody revisits them. A note that is never read again is a note that was written for the act of writing, which is fine, but it is not knowledge.

The cure is not more notes. It is fewer, revisited on purpose, with the source one click away.

That is the whole argument. The rest of this piece is examples.
